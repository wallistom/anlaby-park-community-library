Our story

From closure list to Hull's first community library

When the council could no longer fund it, the people of Anlaby Park did something unusual: they took the library on themselves. It made us the first community-run library in Hull, and we are still the only one.

The arrangement is a genuine partnership. Hull City Council supplies the catalogue and the professional library infrastructure; we supply the building, the volunteers, and the day-to-day running of the place.
