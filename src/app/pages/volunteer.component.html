"A very rewarding experience"

That is how our volunteers describe it, and it is the reason most of them stay for years. You meet your neighbours properly, you learn something, and you can see exactly what your time achieves — it is the building you are standing in.

If you are not sure it is for you, come in for a coffee and ask whoever is on the counter what their week looks like. No obligation whatsoever.

  • Full training, and never thrown in at the deep end
  • Give as much or as little time as suits you
  • Plenty of roles that do not involve the counter at all
  • Free coffee. Obviously.
