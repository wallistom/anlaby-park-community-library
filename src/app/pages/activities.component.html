How do I join one?

You turn up. That really is it — there is no membership, no form, and no minimum commitment. If you would rather check the day and time first, or you would like someone to keep an eye out for you on your first visit, give us a ring and say so.

Want to start something new?

Most of what runs here started because somebody suggested it. If you have an idea for a group — or you already run one and need somewhere to meet — we would like to hear from you.

Independent groups meet here too. The Hull & District Chess Club is a long-standing example.
