What we collect, and why

We only collect information you type into one of our forms. Specifically:

  • Room hire enquiries Your name, email, phone number, organisation, and details of the booking — so we can reply and arrange it.
  • Volunteer enquiries Your name, contact details, availability, and the roles you are interested in — so a trustee can get in touch.
  • Contact messages Your name, email, and message — so we can answer you.

Our lawful basis

We rely on legitimate interests: you have contacted us and expect a reply. We do not run a mailing list, and we never use your details for marketing.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored in Amazon Web Services' London region (eu-west-2) and are readable only by trustees who have signed in with an approved Google account. We do not transfer your data outside the UK, and we never sell or share it with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Trustees signing in to the admin area have a session token stored in their own browser, which is cleared when they sign out or close the tab. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the trustee sign-in uses Google's identity service — both of which will see the visiting IP address as part of serving the request.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to deal with your request and to keep proper charity records, and we review them periodically.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email {{ site.email }} or ring {{ site.phone }} and we will sort it out.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
